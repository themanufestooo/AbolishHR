# Interview Prep

For every interview, build a briefing. Ask your assistant to prepare one a day or two before.

## Company briefing template

- **What they do:** [ONE-PARAGRAPH PLAIN-LANGUAGE SUMMARY]
- **Why this role exists:** [WHAT PROBLEM THE HIRE SOLVES]
- **What they're likely screening for:** [TOP 3 REQUIREMENTS FROM THE POSTING]
- **Your matching evidence:** [YOUR 2–3 STRONGEST RELEVANT FACTS]
- **Questions to ask them:** [3–5 REAL QUESTIONS — about the team, the work, success in the role]

## Likely questions — prepare answers from your verified profile

1. "Tell me about yourself." → 60-second version: where you've been, what you're good at, why this role.
2. "Why do you want to work here?" → the specific, truthful reason (from your cover letter).
3. "Tell me about a challenge you overcame." → a real story: situation, what you did, result.
4. "What's your greatest weakness?" → honest, with what you're doing about it.
5. Role-specific: [ADD 2–3 BASED ON THE POSTING]

**Rule:** practice out loud. The assistant can role-play as the interviewer — but it must clearly label its feedback as opinion, not fact.

## Positioning

Map their needs to your evidence before you walk in:

| They need | Your evidence |
|---|---|
| [REQUIREMENT 1] | [YOUR VERIFIED EXPERIENCE] |
| [REQUIREMENT 2] | [YOUR VERIFIED EXPERIENCE] |

If a requirement has no matching evidence, don't fake it — prepare an honest answer about how you'd close the gap.

## After the interview

Send a thank-you message within 24 hours: thank them for their time, reference one specific thing you discussed, restate your interest briefly. Log the interview and any next steps in your tracker.
