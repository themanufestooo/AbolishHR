# Public Computer Guide

_For libraries, community centers, workforce offices, and any shared computer._

## The core principle

**The shared computer is only a screen. It is never your filing cabinet.**

Your profile, your applications, and your history live in your own account — on your phone or a personal email you control. The library computer just displays them temporarily. When you log out or your session ends, everything is wiped.

## Session rules

- **Never save passwords** on a shared computer. Not in the browser, not in a document.
- **Never leave resumes, ID photos, or personal documents** in Downloads or on the Desktop. If you must upload a file, delete it from the computer afterward.
- **Log out of everything** when you're done — the AI assistant, your email, every job site.
- **Clear the clipboard** (copy a blank space) before you walk away.
- Assume anything left on the machine can be seen by the next person.

## Working in short sessions

Public computers often limit you to 30–60 minutes. The system is built for that:

1. **Your assistant saves progress after every completed step** — a finished form section, a drafted resume, a logged application. You never lose work mid-session.
2. **Resume from anywhere** — pick up on your phone, at home, or at another branch. Your state travels with your account, not the machine.
3. **Phone ↔ computer transfer** — use a QR code or emailed link to move your session between your phone and the terminal. Type as little as possible on the shared keyboard.

## Plain language and accessibility

- The assistant should explain every step in plain language — no jargon.
- Ask for a bigger text size, screen-reader mode, or voice input if you need it. These are normal requests.
- Bilingual support: onboarding and explanations can be in your preferred language, while applications stay in the employer's language.

## The role of staff

Library and center staff are there to help you **access** the system — not to act as recruiters or read your private answers. They can help you:

- Get started and understand what the assistant does
- Recover your session or account
- Scan documents
- Reach a human support channel

What they should **not** see: your application answers, your identity details, or your messages to employers — unless you explicitly choose to share them. Any staff dashboard shows only operational status (e.g. "3 people in onboarding"), never applicant content.

## If something goes wrong

- Locked out of your account? Use your phone/email recovery — never create a second account invisibly.
- Hit a CAPTCHA or identity check the assistant can't pass? That's by design — it hands the step to you (see `../application/HANDOFF_CHECKLIST.md`).
- Confused or stuck? Stop and ask staff or a support channel. Never guess on a legal or identity question.
