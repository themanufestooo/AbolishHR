One row per opportunity. Update it after every action. This is your memory of the search — it prevents duplicate applications and shows what's actually working.

## Weekly review (15 minutes)

1. Any responses? Move them to the next stage.
2. Any follow-ups due? Send them (see `../outreach/OUTREACH_GUIDE.md`).
3. Any scores that feel wrong in hindsight? Tell your assistant so it adjusts.
4. What's your interview rate per 10 quality applications? That's the number that matters — not total applications sent.

## Rules

- Every job URL appears once. Check the tracker before applying anywhere.
- Every submitted application records its resume version (see `../application/RESUME_GUIDE.md`).
- Keep a running history — don't delete rows. A "no response" today is data for next month.
