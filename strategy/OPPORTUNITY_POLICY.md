## Red flags — filter these out

- Pay below your floor with no upside
- Schedule conflicts with your hard constraints
- Requires certifications, licenses, or experience you don't have and can't quickly get
- "Too good to be true" pay for simple work (common scam pattern)
- Asks for money, fees, or banking details to apply
- No verifiable company (no website, no address, no real contact)
- Vague job description that won't say what the work actually is
- Demands you start immediately with no interview process

## Notes

- Part-time and flexible roles get their own search lane — standard full-time listings will otherwise drown them out.
- Revisit this policy every few weeks. Your constraints and goals will change as you learn what the market offers.
