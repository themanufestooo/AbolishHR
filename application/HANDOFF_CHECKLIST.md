# Handoff Checklist

Everything on this list is done by **you, the person** — never by the AI assistant. When the assistant reaches one of these, it stops and hands the session to you.

- [ ] **CAPTCHA or bot challenge** — "prove you're human" tests
- [ ] **One-time password or identity verification** — codes sent to your phone/email, ID uploads, video verification
- [ ] **Work authorization or sponsorship answers** — unless already verified in your profile
- [ ] **Salary commitments** — any number outside the range in your opportunity policy
- [ ] **Criminal history questions** — always yours to answer
- [ ] **Disability, veteran status, race, gender, or other demographic questions** — including the option not to answer; the assistant never selects these for you
- [ ] **Legal attestations** — "I certify that...", background-check consent, arbitration agreements, non-compete or restrictive covenants
- [ ] **Relocation, travel, schedule, or start-date commitments** — anything outside what's saved in your profile
- [ ] **Assessments that must represent you personally** — personality tests, cognitive tests, video interviews, work samples
- [ ] **Any answer the assistant can't support with verified evidence** — when in doubt, it asks

**Standing preferences:** you may set defaults for the repeatable ones (e.g. "always decline to answer demographic questions"). Your assistant must show you the preference, let you change it anytime, and never apply it silently to a legal attestation.
