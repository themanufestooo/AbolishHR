# AbolishHR

> Free, mission-driven templates that turn any AI assistant into a careful, truthful job-search helper.

## Mission

Help people land jobs that fit their profile — without the burden of applying.

Applying for jobs has become a second job: searching the same sites, rewriting the same information, filling in the same forms, over and over. This project gives that time back. It is a free, open set of templates that turns any AI assistant into a careful, truthful job-search helper.

**This project is free forever and mission-driven.** It is not built to make money. It exists to help people — especially people with limited computer access, limited time, or lower confidence with technology — get a fair shot at work that fits them.

## Who it's for

- People applying from public or shared computers (libraries, community centers)
- People with limited time (parents, caregivers, people working hourly jobs)
- People with less confidence using computers
- People with unconventional work histories — gig work, caregiving, informal work, entrepreneurship — that deserves to be translated, never invented
- Anyone who wants a job search based on fit, not on submitting the most applications

## The core idea

Most job-application tools try to submit as many applications as possible. This project does the opposite. It optimizes for **opportunity quality and interview probability**:

1. Build a verified profile of what you have actually done (never invented).
2. Score each opportunity against what you want — only pursue the ones worth it.
3. Tailor each application truthfully to the role.
4. Track everything: applications, outreach, interviews, outcomes.
5. Keep the person in control of every consequential answer.

## Quickstart (3 steps)

**Step 1 — Give your AI agent its instructions.**
Copy `charter/AGENT_CHARTER.md` into your AI assistant as its instructions or system prompt. This tells it how to behave: what to do on its own, what to always ask you about, and what it must never do.

**Step 2 — Complete the onboarding workbook.**
Open `onboarding/ONBOARDING_WORKBOOK.md` and work through it with your agent (about 20 minutes). It builds your career profile and your job strategy. Read `onboarding/CONSENT.md` first so you know exactly what the agent will and won't do.

**Step 3 — Run your search.**
With your profile done, ask your agent to find and score opportunities using `strategy/SCORING_MODEL.md`, then build application packages with the guides in `application/`. Track everything in `tracker/APPLICATION_TRACKER.md`.

## Works with any AI assistant

These are plain text templates — no special software, no install, no account needed. They work with ChatGPT, Claude, Muse, Gemini, or any other AI assistant that can follow instructions. If you switch assistants, your profile and tracker come with you.

## What's in this repo

| Folder | What's inside |
|---|---|
| `charter/` | The agent's instructions: mission, rules, and boundaries |
| `onboarding/` | The intake workbook, profile format, and consent guide |
| `strategy/` | How opportunities get scored and what to pursue |
| `application/` | Resume, cover letter, and answer guides + the human handoff checklist |
| `tracker/` | Application tracker and outcome log templates |
| `outreach/` | Guide to finding people and writing respectful outreach |
| `interview/` | Interview preparation templates |
| `library/` | Guide for using this safely on public/shared computers |

## Important rules

- **Nothing here is submitted automatically without you.** The agent prepares; you decide. Every application is visible to you before it goes anywhere.
- **The agent never invents qualifications.** Only verified experience goes into your materials.
- **Sensitive answers stay yours.** Work authorization, criminal history, disability, and legal attestations are always your decision — the agent hands those to you.
- **Your data stays yours.** On a shared computer, nothing personal is ever stored. See `library/PUBLIC_COMPUTER_GUIDE.md`.
