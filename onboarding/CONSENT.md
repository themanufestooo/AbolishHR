# Consent Guide

Read this before you start. It explains what your AI job-search assistant will do, what it will never do, and what happens to your information.

## What the assistant WILL do

- Help you write down your work history and skills (the onboarding workbook)
- Search for job openings and score them against what you want
- Draft a resume and cover letters using only facts you've confirmed
- Fill in routine form fields (name, contact info, work history) on application sites
- Track your applications, outreach, interviews, and results
- Help you prepare for interviews
- Stop and ask you whenever something is sensitive, uncertain, or legally binding

## What the assistant will NEVER do

- Invent or exaggerate your experience, dates, skills, or education
- Answer questions about work authorization, criminal history, disability, or demographics for you
- Sign, agree to, or submit any legal statement, background-check consent, or binding agreement
- Bypass a CAPTCHA, identity check, or any employer security control
- Submit an application without your explicit approval
- Store your passwords anywhere
- Share your information with anyone except the employers you choose to apply to

## What gets stored, and where

- **Your career profile and job strategy** — stored so the assistant remembers them between sessions. You can review, correct, export, or delete them anytime.
- **Your identity answers** — stored separately from your career profile, used only to fill forms.
- **Your application history** — what you applied to, when, with which resume version, and what happened.
- **Your passwords** — never stored. Not in the profile, not in the chat, nowhere.

## On a shared or public computer

If you're using a library or community computer, extra rules apply — see `../library/PUBLIC_COMPUTER_GUIDE.md`. The short version: the shared computer is only a screen. Your information lives in your own account (on your phone or email). When you log out or your time runs out, everything is cleared.

## Your rights

- See everything the assistant has stored about you
- Correct anything that's wrong
- Export your profile and history
- Delete everything
- Withdraw permission at any time — the assistant stops immediately

**By completing the onboarding workbook, you confirm you've read this and agree to how the assistant works.**
