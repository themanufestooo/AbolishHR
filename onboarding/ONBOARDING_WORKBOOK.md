# Onboarding Workbook

A guided intake that builds your job-search profile. Takes about 20 minutes with your AI assistant. Answer in your own words — short answers are fine. Your assistant will organize them into your profile.

> Before starting, read `CONSENT.md` so you know what your assistant will and won't do.

## Part 1 — Career history

_For each job you've held, most recent first:_

1. Employer name: [EMPLOYER NAME]
2. Your job title: [JOB TITLE]
3. Dates you worked there (month/year to month/year): [START DATE] – [END DATE]
4. What did you actually do day to day? (3–5 plain sentences): [DESCRIPTION]
5. Tools, machines, or software you used: [TOOLS]
6. Anything measurable? (Examples: "served 40 customers a day," "cut delivery time by a week," "managed a team of 5"): [RESULTS]

_Repeat for each employer. Include part-time and temporary work._

## Part 2 — Transferable experience

_Paid jobs aren't the whole story. Tell your assistant about:_

1. **Caregiving** — Have you cared for children, elders, or family members? What did that involve day to day? [DESCRIPTION]
2. **Volunteer work** — Any volunteering, church, school, or community work? [DESCRIPTION]
3. **Gig or informal work** — Driving, deliveries, repairs, cleaning, selling, freelancing? [DESCRIPTION]
4. **Running something of your own** — A business, a side hustle, organizing events? [DESCRIPTION]

_Your assistant's job is to translate this into job-relevant skills without inventing anything. Example: "managed household schedules and appointments for 3 kids" is real scheduling experience._

## Part 3 — Job strategy

1. What kinds of roles are you looking for? (Name 1–3, e.g. "warehouse associate," "customer service rep"): [ROLE 1], [ROLE 2], [ROLE 3]
2. Full-time, part-time, or either? [PREFERENCE]
3. Minimum pay you can accept: [AMOUNT] per [HOUR/WEEK/MONTH/YEAR]
4. Where can you work? (Neighborhoods, cities, max commute): [LOCATION / COMMUTE LIMIT]
5. Remote, in-person, or hybrid? [PREFERENCE]
6. Schedule needs (nights? weekends? school pickup times?): [SCHEDULE NEEDS]
7. Anything you will NOT do? (Examples: overnight shifts, heavy lifting over [WEIGHT], long travel): [HARD NOs]
8. What do you want to learn or grow into? [LEARNING GOALS]

## Part 4 — Identity record (kept separate)

_This is sensitive. It is stored apart from your career profile and only used to fill forms — never to write your resume._

1. Legal full name: [LEGAL NAME]
2. Phone: [PHONE]
3. Email: [EMAIL]
4. City/state/ZIP: [LOCATION]
5. Are you authorized to work in [COUNTRY]? [YES/NO]
6. Will you need sponsorship now or in the future? [YES/NO]
7. Education (school, degree or diploma, year): [EDUCATION]
8. Earliest start date: [DATE]
9. Willing to relocate? [YES/NO/CONDITIONAL]

_Your assistant must never guess these. If you skip one, it stays blank until you provide it._

## Part 5 — Evidence

_List anything that backs up your profile:_

- Certifications or licenses: [NAME, ISSUER, YEAR]
- Portfolio, work samples, or photos of your work: [LINK OR DESCRIPTION]
- References (people who will vouch for you): [NAME, RELATIONSHIP, CONTACT] — only with their permission
- Anything else: [DESCRIPTION]

## Part 6 — Review and approve

Before your first search, your assistant should show you:

1. Your finished career profile — **check every fact. Correct anything wrong.**
2. Three sample job postings with scores — tell it if the scoring feels off.
3. A first-draft resume — **approve every factual claim before it's used anywhere.**

Nothing moves forward until you say so.
